## About

Data Engineer with 4+ years of professional experience across banking and retail (SMBC Indonesia, Bank Sinarmas, Kanmo Group). I build and own ETL/ELT pipelines and data warehousing end to end, with 100% submission accuracy on OJK and Bank Indonesia regulatory reporting.

More recently I have been building AI-augmented data platforms: LLM/RAG agents that validate data against documented business rules, answer questions over the data catalog with cited sources, and turn manual review into automated, auditable pipelines. I hold four Databricks Academy credentials, including AI Agent Fundamentals and Generative AI Fundamentals.

- Based in Jakarta, Indonesia
- Open to relocation (visa sponsorship required)
